**Position:** "Production-ready. Not a whitepaper."

- Live dashboard, real metrics, actual customers

- 8 specialized agents with proven workloads
- SLA guarantees with financial penalties

### vs On-Premise

**Position:** "Cloud economics without cloud costs."

- No CapEx, no hardware maintenance
- Scale up/down instantly
- Global distribution without data centers
